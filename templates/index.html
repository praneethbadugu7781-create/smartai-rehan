How SmartAgri AI Works

Our multi-stage intelligence pipeline transforms soil and weather data into actionable farm profit insights.

01

Farm Data

Enter 7 soil nutrient (N, P, K, pH) and weather parameters (Temp, Humidity, Rain).

02

AI Classifier

Random Forest Machine Learning model calculates exact probability distributions.

03

Crop Ranking

Evaluates and displays Top 3 suitable crops ranked by suitability percentage.

04

Resource Insights

Generates tailored fertilizer guidance, water needs, and soil status reports.

05

Farm Economics

Matches crop yield, prices, and costs to compute estimated revenue & profit/acre.

PROJECT HIGHLIGHTS

Key Architectural Features

Random Forest ML

Trained on multi-dimensional soil & climate records achieving 99.3% prediction accuracy.

Real Top-3 Ranking

Utilizes sklearn `predict_proba()` to present accurate confidence scores without hardcoding.

Decoupled Economics

Separates ML suitability classification from revenue and cost formulas using real CSV data.

Domain Validation

Comprehensive client-side & server-side numeric range enforcement for data integrity.

ACADEMIC & FINAL-YEAR DEMONSTRATION

About SmartAgri AI

SmartAgri AI is an intelligent agricultural decision-support application built for college final-year project demonstration. It unifies machine learning crop prediction with soil health diagnosis, fertilizer management, weather risk interpretation, and farm financial decision support into a cohesive web experience.
